01 / The typeface

A softer way
to make a statement.

Fluma is an organic display typeface with rounded volumes and fine, tapering connections. Its letterforms swell, bend, and settle into a warm rhythm made for short, expressive text.

Designed for headlines, identities, packaging, and other large-scale settings, Fluma has one Regular style and covers Google Fonts Latin Core, including Turkish characters.
