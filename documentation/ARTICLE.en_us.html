Fluma is a liquid organic display typeface by Baturay Kocatepe. Its soft, tapering forms are intended for large typography in headlines, identities, and editorial compositions.

The regular style covers GF Latin Core and Turkish letters such as Ç ç Ğ ğ İ ı Ö ö Ş ş Ü ü. The source was developed in Glyphr Studio and is distributed with a reproducible UFO, TTF, and OTF build.
